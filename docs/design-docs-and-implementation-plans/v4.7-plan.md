# v4.7 Where you stand, and EarnKit speaks first: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A builder never has to know what to ask.
- "Where do I stand" gets a real answer: the page's own score and rank, money, and the next deadline.
- The first reply is about money only.
- Every step says why in one line.
- Every number matches everywhere.
- EarnKit reaches the builder first: by email, and when a session starts.

**Architecture:** One source per number:
- **Score and rank:** `bench-data.ts` / `standingOf` and `shownRankLine`, the same functions the page uses.
- **Money total:** `pool.ts openMoneyUsd`, the same figure `/api/stats` reports.
- **Deadlines:** a new `deadline.ts` that formats `ends_at` in the program's own time zone.

Replies, the site and email all read those. Asks gain a `why` line. Email reuses `digest.ts` (Resend, `digest_subscriptions`) and adds a daily alert send with its own sent-log. The MCP instructions tell agents to call `find` when a session starts.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, TypeScript, Postgres, node:test via tsx. Resend, already configured (`RESEND_API_KEY` is set; earnkit.com DNS added 2026-10-30), on its free tier (caps in `free-tier.ts`).

**Spec:** the owner's asks of 2026-10-30. The owner agreed to all three decisions: show "77 · #1" in replies, use one money figure, and set up the email domain.
- "When I asked where do I stand it never actually told me."
- "EarnKit never messages me, alerts me or anything … What should I ask? Most users won't care."
- The three points:
  1. **The first reply is money only.** It opens with the best-fit program, its amount and deadline, and the one step to qualify. The Stripe ask moves into grant drafts, and the token is offered only once the money step is done.
  2. **EarnKit gets a voice at every step.** For example: "Shipping this week keeps your 6-week streak and lifts your score."
  3. **Every number matches.** "77 · #1" in replies, one money total, and deadlines in each program's own time zone. The "EarnKit doesn't use points" note goes.
- The earlier list also covers:
  - The Stripe ask only reaches builders whose target program cares about revenue.
  - llms.txt token wording.
  - No automatic token on wallet proof.

## Exact copy

- **Standing** (`standing` fact, and the reply's standing paragraph). Parts are joined with `. `, and each part shows only when it is true:
  - `Score {score} · {rankWords}`. `rankWords` is `shownRankLine` as the page shows it: `#1 of 40 builders` or `Top 4% of builders`. When the pool is under `RANK_MIN_POOL` it is `Score {score}`.
  - `{N} weeks shipping in a row` (or `1 week shipping`).
  - `{$X} won so far` from recorded outcomes, or `No grants won yet` when there are none.
  - `{K} applications in, {D} drafts ready`, each half only when non-zero.
  - `Next: {program} closes {day}` (the earliest open deadline among their fits).
- **Why lines** (the line just before the ask):
  - `ship`: `Shipping this week keeps your {N}-week streak and lifts your score.` With no streak: `Shipping this week starts your streak and lifts your score.`
  - `checkin`: `A Monday check-in keeps your drafts current before deadlines.`
  - `share`: `Builders who share wins get noticed by programs.`
  - `post`: `A post with real numbers is what reviewers read.`
  - `qualify`: keeps its own head and gap paragraphs, no new line.
  - `apply`: keeps its own, no new line.
- **Email ask** (replaces `EMAIL_QUESTION`): `Want deadline alerts and a Monday summary by email? Reply with the address.`
- **Alert emails:**
  - Closing:
    - Subject: `{program} closes {day}`
    - Body:
      - `{program} closes {day}, {amount}.`
      - `Your draft is ready in your agent.` when a draft exists, else `Ask your agent to draft it.`
  - Accepted:
    - Subject: `You got into {program}`
    - Body: `You got into {program}.` plus the share link line from `outcomeShareLine`.
  - New fit:
    - Subject: `New: {program}, {amount}`
    - Body:
      - `{program} opened, {amount}, {when}.`
      - `{project} fits it. Ask your agent to draft it.`
- **Session-start instruction** (in `BENCH_INSTRUCTION_LINES`, replacing line 1's opening): `Call find at the start of every session, before the builder asks, and whenever they ask about money, grants or where they stand.` The rest of line 1 stays.
- **First-run "what to ask" line** (the setup-done reply, a statement): `Ask me anytime: where do I stand, what can I apply to, or draft my application.`

## Review Focus

1. A builder with a rank pool under `RANK_MIN_POOL` sees `Score 77` with no rank, and so does the page. The two are never different.
2. Deadlines:
   - A program whose deadline is 11:59 PM PT on Nov 1 shows Nov 1 on the site, in replies and in email, never Nov 2.
   - A program with no stated zone falls back to UTC in all three.
3. The first ranked reply to a builder with an open fit leads with that program's amount and deadline. No Stripe, token, check-in or ship ask comes before it.
4. Proving a wallet never launches a token, even for a builder whose `token_offered` was set by the old v4.0 setup step.
5. An alert email is never sent twice for the same event.
   - The total stays within the shared Resend day cap, with the digest taking priority on Mondays.
   - An unsubscribed address gets nothing.
