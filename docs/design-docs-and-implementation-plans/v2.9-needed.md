# v2.9: Needed, not optional

Status: approved (owner, 2026-10-08). Builds on v2.8.1.

Source: four read-only audits run 2026-10-08 (bypass test, matching, hosting and gas, revenue). Goals, in order: installs, then token launches. Persona: a lazy builder who only wants money. The bypass test showed an agent alone gets about 80% of EarnKit's value in 10 minutes; v2.9 adds what an agent alone can't do: accurate fit, a token any agent can launch, a live link, money that arrives by itself, and alerts that arrive without opening the agent.

Paths are relative to `apps/frontend/lib/earnkit/`. Exceptions: `app/` and `db/` are under `apps/frontend/`; `programs/` is at the repo root.

## Owner decisions (binding)

- **Gas:** EarnKit sponsors gas for token launches only (tokens.fun already pays it). No wallet top-ups and no gas drip, ever.
- **Hosting:** approved, on earnkit.com subdomains (no new domain). A site lives at most until 7 days after its program ends.
- **No extra money:** every new piece runs inside a free tier, with hard caps in code so a bill can never start.
- **Alerts:** a weekly email, not daily: new programs, wins by EarnKit builders, and the builder's own deadlines.
- **Money:** no bonus pool seeding. No EarnKit-funded campaigns until there are external case studies or a co-sponsor. The split stays: builder 40%, Safe 30%, builder bonus 20%, MINI 10%.
- **UX rule:** the token is the only skippable step. No reply ever asks the builder to skip anything else.
- **Public /programs pages stay as they are.** No shareable or stakeholder pages. The owner is the only decider.

## Changes

### A. Matching accuracy

1. **Owned repos only.**
   - `ownerOk(owner, builder)` (pure, `match.ts`): false for any owner in `EARNKIT_ORGS = ['earnkitai']` (next to `KITS` in `programs.ts`), always, owner test installs included. True when the owner equals `github_user`.
   - A repo owned by someone else counts only with at least 5 commits by the builder that are at least 30% of its last 100 commits (one extra call, at most 3 candidates). One commit is no longer enough (`match-projects.ts` `contributedRepos`).
   - Forks, templates and archived repos are dropped before any per-repo call.
   - Local repos whose remote owner is in `EARNKIT_ORGS` are dropped. The `mcp.ts` instruction becomes "report repos the builder wrote; skip clones of other people's code".
   - One repo key (`owner/repo`) across GitHub and local results, so a repo never appears twice.
2. **Real-product score.** `productScore(facts)` (pure, `match.ts`), 6 signals: `active` (builder commit in 90 days), `young` (created in 365 days, or active), `original` (not fork or template, name not a tutorial pattern), `readme` (1.5 KB or more, not a framework default), `deployed` (verified link or contract, item 5), `owned` (item 1).
   - Tiers: **product** = 5 or more with `active`, `owned` and `deployed`; **project** = 3 to 4; **noise** = 2 or less.
   - Noise gets no row, no match and no alert. Project caps at `after_step` and names the missing signal. Only product can be `likely`.
   - Score at most 8 repos per call (today up to 30). Cached in a new `product jsonb` column on `github_repo_facts`.
   - `any_chain` programs (a16z) match product-tier repos only, one repo shown, at most `after_step` for `investment: true` ("apply as a company: needs a full-time team"). New program field `min_product_score` (a16z: 5), checked by `programs:check`.
   - At most 3 repos per program in `matches` (`tools.ts`).
3. **Builder memory: `tell_earnkit`.** New tool (not read-only). It absorbs `report_outcome`, so the tool count stays the same.
   - Inputs: `program_status` (`applied`, `not_interested`, `won`, `placed`, `lost`), `repo_facts` (`mainnet_chains`, `testnet_chains`, `live_url`, `has_users`, `not_mine`).
   - New tables `builder_program_status` and `builder_repo_facts` (schema as in the matching audit, section 3), read once per `list_programs`.
   - A statement that lowers eligibility always wins: testnet-only clears that chain's mainnet; `not_mine` drops the repo; `not_interested` removes the program everywhere (table, offer, alerts, also qualifies); `applied` removes it until the program's next round and shows it in "Your entries" as "applied outside EarnKit".
   - A statement that raises eligibility is a claim: it moves a row to `after_step` "confirm", never to `likely`, until item 5 verifies it. `has_users` clears `real_users` but keeps the row at Medium or harder.
   - Outcomes keep v2.8 rules: only `basis = 'announcement'` counts toward any public figure.
4. **Difficulty = max(program odds, distance).** `rowDifficulty(card, verdict)` (pure, `guide.ts`).
   - Distance: none or minutes = Easy; hours or days = Medium; weeks = Hard.
   - `real_users` missing is never Easy: Hard while unverified, Medium after a `has_users` claim or a verified live link.
   - A program tagged `needs a live product` is at least Medium at program level, even with an EarnKit rating. `programs:check` rejects Easy plus that tag.
   - Investment programs, or acceptance under 1%, are Hard.
   - Used for the matched table cell, the sort and the opening offer (`present.ts`). Program-level difficulty stays on the landing page and guides.
   - Testnet-only repos: `live_url` is no longer "minutes"; the effort is `hours` (mainnet deploy first).
5. **Link and contract verification.** New `verify.ts`.
   - `placeholderHost(host)` (pure): RFC 2606/6761 names (`example.com/net/org`, `*.example`, `*.test`, `*.invalid`, `*.localhost`, `*.local`), a short list (`yourdomain.com`, `yoursite.com`, `domain.com`, `test.com`, `myapp.com`), IP literals, `earnkit.com` and `github.com/earnkitai`. Applied in `canonicalBuilderUrl` (`untrusted-text.ts`), `reportedUrl` and to GitHub homepages. Builder sees: "Use your real site, not a placeholder."
   - Safe probe: `safeProbe` (`safe-fetch.ts`) on the live link, at most 5 per `list_programs` and 1 per entry step. A 2xx sets `live_verified`.
   - Contract check: one `eth_getCode` per address on the program chain's mainnet through one `rpcFor(chain)` map (`BASE_RPC_URL` exists; add public defaults for Arc, Monad, Tempo, Avalanche, Robinhood). `0x` or an EIP-7702 delegation means not deployed. At most 2 per repo, 6 per call.
   - New table `url_checks (key text PRIMARY KEY, ok boolean, checked_at timestamptz)`: pass cached 24 h, failure 1 h. Keys `url:<url>` and `getcode:<chain>:<address>`.
   - Program field `needs_contract: <chain>` (Arc Microgrants: `arc`). Its live link is complete only when the probe passes and a contract on that mainnet has code.
   - `entryMissing` (`token-launch.ts`) treats an unverified live link as missing for mainnet requirements; `hasLiveProduct` (`tools.ts`) becomes `live_verified`. A failed check never blocks; it shows one fix line ("Live link didn't load from here. Check it's public.") and the entry is not complete.
6. **Base is no longer Easy.** Remove the EarnKit Easy rating from `programs/base-builder-grants.json`. Base is Medium at program level and Hard on rows missing real users (item 4).
7. **Alert precision rule.** A new program is alerted (in replies and the weekly email, item 19) only when, for one remembered owned repo: the tier fits (product for `any_chain` or investment, project or better otherwise); the verdict is `likely`, or `after_step` with effort of hours or less and no `real_users` or `new_work` missing; the row is not Hard (unless $10,000+ per winner and product tier); the program is not entered, applied or not interested; 3 or more days remain.
   - `installations.fit_chains` is replaced by `installations.fit_repos jsonb` (up to 5 compact repo facts), so alerts make no GitHub call. The `p.easy ||` clause in `alerts.ts` is removed.
8. **Outcome feedback loop** (inside `programs:sync`, no new cron).
   - New table `match_shown` (installation, program, repo, status, product_score, row_level, day), one row per shown row per day.
   - Per program, over announced outcomes only, compute `match_floor` (lowest winning `product_score`) and `match_precision` (wins or places / entries from `likely` rows). Written only at 30 or more labelled entries (`WIN_RATE_MIN_LABELLED`); dormant until then.
   - A repo below `match_floor` is at most `after_step`. A program under 5% precision shows one step harder.

### B. Ownership proof and the token offer

9. **Sign in with GitHub** as ownership proof, so hosted agents (Grok Bot) can launch.
   - A GitHub OAuth App with no scopes (public data only). Route `app/github/verify` with a one-time, 15-minute nonce bound to the entry and the repo, never to the installation alone (shared hosted clients, v2.8.1 item 17).
   - Ownership passes when the signed-in login equals the repo owner, or the authenticated repo read returns `permissions.push` or `admin` for that user. If a scopeless token doesn't return permissions for org repos, org repos fall back to the proof line.
   - Stored in a new table `github_verifications (repo_key, login, project_id, verified_at)`. `token-gate.ts` accepts it in place of the `earnkit.txt` line. `confirm.ts` no longer returns `record_only` for `can_push: false`; it returns the sign-in link.
   - Coding agents that can push keep the proof line (zero clicks). The link is only for agents that can't push, or after a proof-line miss.
   - Builder sees: "One click to prove TipJar is yours, then $TIP launches: <link>"
   - Identity linking (v2.8.1 item 18) also accepts a GitHub verification as proof.
10. **Token offered right after the match.** The first reply's closing offer becomes the token offer for the best owned, product-tier fit:
    - "TipJar fits Arc Microgrants: $500. Launch $TIP (TipJar) on Base now? You earn 40% of its trading fees, plus a shot at the builder bonus. yes / skip"
    - On yes, `launch_token` creates a draft entry for that program and launches. The bonus counts once the entry is submitted and complete (existing rules). The wallet is asked in the same line only if none is known; a remembered wallet is never suggested on shared clients.
    - On skip, the token is offered once more at confirmation (v2.8 Yes line), then never again for that repo.
    - "40%" still appears once per entry flow: at whichever offer the builder answers.
11. **Only the token is skippable.** Every other "skip" leaves builder text. Optional coach questions are not asked at all; only required fields are asked. `confirm.ts` keeps `decision: 'skip'` for the token only.

### C. Hosting and gas

12. **Static hosting on earnkit.com.**
    - Sites live at `<project>-<user>.earnkit.com` (one level, so the free Cloudflare Universal SSL covers it). One Cloudflare Worker (free plan) on `*.earnkit.com/*` maps the `Host` header to the R2 prefix `<slug>/` and caches with the Cache API.
    - Reserved names never route to the Worker: `www`, `docs`, `api`, `mcp`, `app`, `mail`, `admin`, `status`, plus every existing DNS record. Explicit DNS records for those keep their current targets.
    - Isolation from earnkit.com: every response carries `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox allow-scripts allow-forms allow-popups allow-modals` (opaque origin: no cookies, no storage of earnkit.com, no same-site requests). EarnKit's own cookies move to the `__Host-` prefix so a subdomain can't set or overwrite them.
    - The sandbox blocks browser storage, so an app that needs `localStorage` must fall back to memory; the upload check tells the agent in one line when the code uses it.
    - Reputation risk, stated once: a bad site on a subdomain can get earnkit.com flagged. Item 14's scan, review and kill switch are the guard.
    - Upload: the agent gets a one-time URL from EarnKit and POSTs one zip to `app/api/host/upload` on the existing Railway service. The server unpacks, checks and writes to R2 with EarnKit's key. No vendor token reaches the agent.
    - The hosted link counts as the live link and goes through item 5 like any other.
    - Builder sees: "TipJar is live: tipjar-alice.earnkit.com"
13. **Limits.**
    - Gate: GitHub sign-in (item 9), account 30 days or older with at least 1 public repo.
    - 3 live sites per GitHub account, 1 per repo (a new deploy overwrites), 10 deploys a day.
    - 25 MB per site, 500 files, 10 MB per file. Static extensions only: html, css, js, json, images, fonts, wasm, txt, xml, webmanifest. Archives and executables refused.
    - Temporary: a site lives until 7 days after its program's end date (the latest end date among the programs its repo entered), then its files are deleted and a "this site has ended" page is served. A site with no entry is deleted 7 days after upload.
    - Free-tier caps in code: total stored under 8 GB (R2 free: 10 GB); new deploys are refused above it ("Hosting is full right now; try again tomorrow"). The Worker free plan stops at 100k requests a day; it never bills.
14. **Abuse rules.**
    - Worker headers: `Content-Security-Policy: form-action 'self'`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.
    - Footer badge: "Built with EarnKit by @alice. Report abuse." (also an install path).
    - Upload scan (regex): a password input next to wallet or bank brand words, "seed phrase" or "recovery phrase", and approval-to-hardcoded-spender patterns go to owner review, not live.
    - Kill switch: one flag per slug, checked by the Worker. `abuse@earnkit.com` and an owner takedown endpoint.
    - Static only. No server code, no custom domains in v2.9.
15. **Gas.** Token launches stay gas-free through tokens.fun. No drip, no top-up, no paymaster. Contract deploys remain the builder's own.

### D. Revenue: automatic claims

16. **Claim all four recipients hourly** inside the existing `claimFees` (`bonus-ledger.ts`), same cron, same payout wallet, same `worthIt()` 10x gas floor. The loop grows from `[bonus wallet, Safe]` to `[bonus wallet, Safe, each EarnKit token's creator recipient, MINI recipient]`.
    - Recipients are read once per token from the launch protocol's locker and cached on `projects`. If that read isn't available, the MINI recipient falls back to a constant in `policy.ts` next to `FEE_SPLIT_BPS`.
    - `claim(owner, WETH)` always pays the owner, so EarnKit never holds the creator's or MINI's money. Creator claims go straight to the builder's wallet.
17. **Gas-out alert.**
    - Each run reads the payout wallet's balance first; under `GAS_WALLET_MIN_WEI` (default 0.002 ETH) it adds a `TOP_UP` line to `needs_owner`.
    - `sweepToSafe`'s gas-out message gets the `TOP_UP` sentinel; `cron.ts` also scans `claim` and `collect` results for "needs ETH for gas".
    - Uses the existing 503, GitHub Actions failure and email path. No new service.
18. **Logging, no double count.** New table `fee_claims (tx_hash, owner, role, weth, gas_wei, at)` with role `safe | bonus | creator | mini`.
    - It is an ops log, never revenue. Revenue stays deposit-based in tokens.fun `fee_events` (Safe slot only), which jenil.ai's `get_revenue` already reads.
    - `fees_to_safe_eth` in `/api/stats` is unchanged and stays labelled "never added again". Creator and MINI claims are not added to any revenue figure (buyback is not revenue).

### E. Alerts

19. **Weekly email, opt-in only.**
    - Asked once, after an entry is recorded: "Get new programs and wins by email, once a week? yes / no". The email from the coach is reused only after that yes; on shared clients it is named in the question.
    - Content, in this order: new programs that fit (item 7), wins by EarnKit builders (announced only, item 20), the builder's own deadlines and decision dates in the coming week. Empty emails are never sent.
    - Sent by the existing hourly cron, Mondays from 14:00 UTC, via Resend's free tier (100 a day, 3,000 a month). Over the daily cap, the rest go on the next run that day or the next day; never over the monthly cap. One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe`).
    - New table `digest_subscriptions (installation_id, email, opted_in_at, unsubscribed_at, last_sent_at)`.
    - Example: "New: Avalanche Mini Grant fits TipJar, $10,000, closes Nov 2."
20. **"Builders using EarnKit won $X".** Shown on the landing page and the first reply only when at least one announced (`basis = 'announcement'`) won or placed outcome with a known prize exists. $X is the sum of those prizes. Self-reports never count. Before that, the line is absent, not zero.

### F. Simplify

21. **One money figure.** One function, `openMoneyUsd()` in `stats.ts`: the top prize per open program, summed, live from the catalog. Shown rounded down ("$1.1M+") on the landing page, docs, `llms.txt`, the first reply and `/api/stats`. The hard-coded "$1.3M+" and "$1,170,500" go. docs.earnkit.com reads `/api/stats`.
22. **One legal page.** `/policy`, `/risk-disclosure` and `/sponsor-terms` merge into `/terms` with anchors; the old paths 301 there. Footer: "EarnKit is a product of Tokenfi Inc."
23. **Dormant self-serve sponsor surfaces leave builder navigation** (/fund, /award links) until escrow is deployed. The routes stay. The docs sponsor page ("EarnKit builder campaigns: talk to us") stays.
24. **MINI on builder pages:** one plain line, "10% buys MINI, EarnKit's token", wherever the split is shown. Split unchanged.
25. **Shorter tool descriptions.** `confirm_entry` and `sponsor_campaign` descriptions are cut to under 800 characters each; rules move into replies' `agent_note`.

## Owner go-live steps

- Create the R2 bucket and the router Worker (both free plan, on the earnkit.com zone); add the R2 key to EarnKit's Railway variables.
- Create the GitHub OAuth App (callback `https://earnkit.com/github/verify/callback`); add its id and secret.
- Create a Resend account, add its DNS records for earnkit.com on Cloudflare, add the key.
- Set `GAS_WALLET_MIN_WEI` if 0.002 ETH is wrong.

## Testing

- **Each item has a regression test**, using the existing fakes (`ctx.matchProjects`, injectable `safe-fetch` resolver, injectable RPC).
- **Assertions:**
  - an `earnkitai/*` repo, a fork, a template and a one-commit contributed repo never produce a row;
  - a COVID-tracker tutorial and a docs repo produce no a16z row; a16z shows at most one repo;
  - `matches` has at most 3 repos per program;
  - `https://example.com`, `yourdomain.com` and an IP literal are refused as live links; a probe failure leaves the entry not complete;
  - an Arc address with `0x` code fails `needs_contract: arc`; one with code passes;
  - a row with `real_users` missing is never Easy; `programs:check` fails on Easy plus `needs a live product`; Base is not Easy;
  - after `applied`, the program leaves the table, offer, also qualifies and alerts until a new round; after `not_interested`, it never returns;
  - a testnet-only claim beats a mainnet string in code; a `has_users` claim never yields `likely`;
  - the first reply ends with the token offer; "skip" appears in builder text only in a token offer; "40%" appears once per entry flow;
  - with `can_push: false`, confirmation returns the sign-in link, never `record_only`; a nonce is single-use, expires at 15 minutes and can't verify a different repo;
  - hosting refuses a 26 MB zip, a `.exe`, a fourth site and an account under 30 days; a flagged slug serves the kill page; every response has the CSP header;
  - `claimFees` claims creator and MINI only above `worthIt()`; a low balance yields `TOP_UP` in `needs_owner` and a 503; `fee_claims` rows never change `fees_to_safe_eth`;
  - the weekly email is never sent without opt-in, never empty, at most once a week, never over the free caps, and carries `List-Unsubscribe`;
  - a site's files are gone 7 days after its program's end date; a reserved name (`docs`, `www`) never reaches the Worker; every hosted response has the sandbox CSP; storage over 8 GB refuses a deploy;
  - the "won $X" line is absent with only self-reported wins;
  - `openMoneyUsd()` is the only money total: no page, doc or reply contains another;
  - builder text never contains a tool name, an emoji, an em dash, "coin", "vault" or "Clanker" (extend `copy-review.test.ts`).
- **The v2.8.1 journey simulation is re-run**, plus a Grok Bot journey that ends in a launched token.

## Out of scope

- Seeding builder bonus pools.
- EarnKit-funded campaigns, until external case studies or a co-sponsor exist.
- Any change to the fee split (builder stays at 40%).
- Wallet gas top-ups, gas drips and paymasters.
- Changes to the public /programs pages.
- Shareable or stakeholder pages.
- Backend or SSR hosting, custom domains, and an auto-ingested "also open" tier.
