## 1. Goal

The owner opens EarnKit in Grok Bot and every reply hands them money: the best program for their repo with a finished draft, one yes away. Monday brings one email and one reply with the week, a ready X post and the money action. Everything that is not money or rank is off the visible surface.

## 3. P0: the owner as power user

### 3.1 The runbook pass (owner in Grok Bot)
The owner runs runbook steps 5 to 8 on production and pastes every reply. Each friction is fixed first, in its own small commit on this branch, before the work below ships. I cannot run Grok Bot as the owner.

### 3.2 Every ranked reply leads with money
- A new first line of the ranked reply, above "Where you stand": `Best fit: <title>, <prize>, <closes Nov 18 | rolling>. <why>` (the top apply candidate, investments included). With no fit, no line.
- The apply step includes rolling programs. Pick order: a fit closing within 30 days (deadline order), else the first rolling fit by `byRelevance` (Base first, grants before investments). Rolling ask: `<title> is open now (rolling). Want me to draft your application from your ship log?`
- Question order becomes connect, apply, backing, share, ship. Apply is money to the builder; backing waits one reply.
- The drafted application ("Apply with this page"): `prepare` in draft-only mode for every program. The draft is the ship log block, then each form field with its answer drafted from the ship log and page, the page link in the first free-text answer, then how to send it:
  - Base Builder Grants: one prefilled Google Form link (the reply's one link), with `primary_challenge` added to the field map.
  - Circle, Alliance ALL19, others: copy and paste per field, the form link as the reply's one link.
  - The one question after a draft: `Tell me once you've sent it.` A yes records `applied` (`tell program_status`), which drives "Applied to X" on the page and the 14-day outcome ask.
- Builder Codes: for Base Builder Grants, EarnKit reads the repo (through the App) for `ox/erc8021`, `Attribution.toDataSuffix`, `dataSuffix` or the `8021...8021` marker. The fit line and the draft say `Builder Codes found in <repo>.` or `First step for Base: add Builder Codes (docs.base.org/base-chain/builder-codes).`

### 3.3 Monday
- The Monday reply drafts the X post, ready to paste: the post text in a quote block, with the page URL inside it as the reply's one link (no intent link). The question stays `Does last week look right?`.
- The Monday email gains, above the week: the money action (`Your <program> draft is ready. It closes in 9 days.` or `<program> is open (rolling), and your draft is one yes away.`) and backing events (`2 new backers this week.`), each only when true. New backers are counted from a `first_seen_at` timestamp added to `coin_backers` (additive column, default now()).
