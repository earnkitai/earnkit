Money math (assumes a 1% swap fee): the Safe gets 1% x 80% (after Clanker) x 30% = 0.24% of a coin's trading
volume. $5,000 a month needs about $2.1M of monthly volume across EarnKit coins. Thirty small builder coins at a few
hundred dollars of volume each produce cents. One coin with real attention produces the target. So the money plan is a
volume plan, and volume follows attention, not launch count.

### Nikita Bier (product)

1. **The product has no heartbeat.** Retention comes from a push the user did not ask for at the moment it matters.
   EarnKit had zero working pushes: the email could not send and the check-in was lost after one unanswered ask. The
   founder not coming back was correct behaviour, not a motivation problem. Tonight's fixes give EarnKit one heartbeat
   (Monday email, plus the agent's own Monday routine). Measure it as one number: Monday opens to Looks right taps.
2. **Answer, then ask.** The first reply does lead with money ($5,000 from Base), but after it every question
   Maya asks ("How much money can I get? I really need money now", "How am I doing?", the next day) gets the same
   GitHub step back as EarnKit's text; the answer is left to the agent. A money-motivated user hears a nag. Each reply
   should carry one line that answers in money ("Up to $5,000 once MealMate's code is on GitHub"), then the one link.
3. **One question per session, and only money questions.** 24 ask kinds (titles, wallet, check-in, email, ship,
   qualify, backing, new repo...) means the builder answers EarnKit's questions instead of EarnKit answering theirs.
   Keep three: connect, the coin (money), the weekly post (distribution). Everything else is a default, not a question.
4. **The growth loop is the weekly post.** It is the only surface where a non-user sees EarnKit. It must be one tap
   from the Monday email (done tonight) and carry the builder's own number (streak, users, money), never points.
5. **Points are noise to a money-motivated user.** "+90 points" in every reply competes with dollars. Show dollars
   (open money, fees earned) or nothing.

### Elon (first principles, delete, integrate)

The algorithm: question every requirement, delete, simplify, accelerate, automate, in that order.

**Delete (proposals for the owner; nothing deleted tonight):**

| Part | Why delete |
|---|---|
| Points (ledger, every reply's last line) | Not money, not shown on pages, adds a line to every reply |
| Backing / backers (coin_backers 0 rows) | A second coin concept next to "the coin"; zero use |
| Sponsor campaigns + escrow scoring (campaigns.ts, sponsor*, ~3,000 lines) | Dormant since v2.2; still on Blockscout; no sponsor |
| Hosting (host-worker, ~1,100 lines) | Never deployed |
| Starters / EarnKit-builds-for-you remnants | Owner ruling: EarnKit never builds for builders |
| The programs table in replies | One best fit with its next step beats a 4-row table |
| Most of the 24 ask kinds | See Nikita 3 |

**Simplify:** one push (Monday), one money line, one link per action, one question. **Accelerate:** the Monday email
sends the hour the week is written. **Automate:** the post drafts itself (done), the claim of fees is automatic (done).

**Vertical integration (the real advantage):** EarnKit owns the whole chain from code to cash: the agent (MCP), the
proof (GitHub App, wallet), the score, the launch rail (tokens.fun), the fee split (Safe 30%), and the post. No
competitor owns all of it. The missing link is attention: integrate the coin into the weekly post card (the coin's
price and the builder's fees on the OG card), so every shipped week markets its coin. One more integration: EarnKit's
own coin as the reference coin. The owner said no to it on 2026-10-19; first principles say the founder's own coin,
posted every week with real shipping, is the single highest-volume coin EarnKit can launch. That is the owner's call.
