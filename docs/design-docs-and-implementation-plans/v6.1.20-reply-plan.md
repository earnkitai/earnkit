# v6.1.20: the reply reads like a person (one task)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan.

**Goal:** The owner's verdict on live v6.1.19 (2026-11-13): "the responses are so ill formatted and the UX is horrible ... confusing, hard to read or simply don't engage in a conversation and no conversions". Every builder-facing reply from find and track is rewritten into plain sentences a tired builder reads in one pass: one opening sentence that says the state, one full sentence per program, blank lines between blocks, no headings, no counts in parentheses, no `First:` / `Their rule:` / `You give:` labels, no quoted commands, in-person and the long tail folded into one short sentence, and a closing that is one short instruction.

**Architecture:** `apps/frontend/lib/v5/reply.ts` (`replyParts`, the block builders, `closingLine`, `gapLine`, `readyLine`, `inPersonLine`, `moreInFull`, the one-Say order), `reply-words.ts`, `lib/v6/reply-words.ts`, `find.ts` (the one-program reply, the open list, the full list, `signInLine`, `APPLY_OFFER` / `TAIL_OFFER` gates), `track.ts` (the lines object), `tool-text.ts` (agent notes stay as they are). **Spec:** this file. Source: the owner's words above and the live replies in the session scratchpad `journeys/lane1m/run*.json`.

## Global Constraints
- No emoji, no em dash; never score, rank, grade, points, coin, token, streak; every builder line ends with a full stop; never a tool, field or file name; money first, then the gap, then one action; one `Say` per reply and it is last; one sign-in sentence per reply; never an apply offer before sign-in or on an unconfirmed repo (the gates `APPLY_OFFER` and `TAIL_OFFER` are re-derived for every new form and tested signed out, unconfirmed and linked); the default reply within 10 lines (blank lines do not count); never an invented fact: the opening acknowledges only what the builder gave (name, a live link, a repo, users reported).
- Commit messages start with `v6.1.20:`. Version 6.1.20 in package.json and the mcp.test pin.

## The reply grammar (default find, with a project)

1. **Opening sentence**, one line, says the state and names the project when a name was given:
   - ready ones: `For {name}, {n} program{s} {is|are} ready to send now.` (n ≥ 1)
   - nothing ready, steps exist: `Nothing is ready to send for {name} yet. The closest:`
   - nothing at all: `Nothing fits {name} yet. Tell me more about it, or its repo link, and I look again.` (this is the whole reply; no Say)
   - no name given: drop `For {name},` / `for {name}` and capitalise.
2. **Blank line, then the programs**, numbered `1.` upward in the order the JSON numbers them, one full sentence each:
   - ready: `{n}. {short title} ({money}). {what EarnKit does}.` where the second sentence is the existing action: `I apply once you say yes.` / `I draft it for you to send.` / `I add the funding file to your repo, then apply.`
   - one step (a real gap): `{n}. {short title} ({money}) needs {gap words} first.` with `{, closes in N days}` inside the parentheses after the money when within 14 days.
   - one rule: `{n}. {short title} ({money}) needs one yes from you: {rule in plain words}.`
   - several steps: `{n}. {short title} ({money}) needs {first gap words}, then {k} more step{s}.`
   - `{short title}`: the catalog title with the organiser's long suffix cut at the first colon or ` - `, max 48 characters, else the full title (pure helper `shortTitle`, pinned for the 52 files: `PayPal AI Hackathon`, `HeroDevs Open Source Sustainability Fund` stays since no colon; a `short_title` catalog field wins when present; add it to README as optional).
   - gap words: the existing `what` texts, lower-case, without a leading article when the sentence already carries one (`needs real users`, `needs the code in a public repo`, `needs a demo video link`, `needs a live repo`).
   - rule words: the rule note as it is, lower-case first letter.
   - the "also" marks (`you_send`, held, claimed by another agent) stay as a trailing clause: `, which you send yourself.` / `, on hold until you say go.` / `, started by your other agent.`
3. **Blank line, then one "Also" sentence** when anything is hidden or in person: `Also open: {in-person titles with money, each "(…)"}, in person with long odds{; and N more in the full list}{; Meta VR closes in 6 days}.` Each part only when present; `Also open:` becomes `Also:` when only the count is left: `Also: 19 more in the full list.`
4. **Blank line, then the closing**, one line, one Say, last:
   - ready, linked: `Say yes and I apply to {short title}.` / `... and I apply to all {k}.` / mixed as today but with short titles and no quotes.
   - ready, signed out: `Sign in at {link} so I know the repo is yours, then say yes and I apply to {short title}.` (unchanged rule; short title)
   - draft paths: `... and I draft it for you to send.` (unchanged)
   - rule yes: `Say {n} if the {short title} rule fits you and I draft it.` (bare number, no quotes; `{n}` is that row's number)
   - users question: `Do real people use it? Say yes and I check.` (signed-out variant: `Do real people use it? Say yes, sign in at {link}, and I check.`)
   - a step the builder makes: `Tell me when {step clause} and I look again.` (unchanged)
   - the sent ask: `Did you send {short title}? Say sent and I note it.` (unchanged)
   - started / not-sent / standing-approval / hold lines: unchanged wording, short titles.
   - the one-rule Next line after a send: `Next: {short title} ({money}) needs one yes: {rule}. Say yes if that fits and I apply.`
5. The results question, the since line, the report lines, the repo notes keep their sentences but sit between the programs and the closing, each on its own line.

## The one-program reply
`{short title} ({money}{, closes ...}). {state sentence}.` then a blank line and the closing. State sentences: ready `It is ready: {what EarnKit does}.`; one gap `It needs {gap words} first.`; rule `It needs one yes from you: {rule}.`; several `It needs {a}, {b} and {c}.` (every gap named, rules last as `and your yes that {rule}`); in person `You attend in person.` before the ask; closed as today.

## The full list (filter.all)
Opening `Everything open for {name}, nearest first:` then the numbered sentences in three runs separated by blank lines with one-line lead-ins `Ready:` / `Not ready yet:` / `In person:` only when a run is non-empty; no counts; the closing as the default's.

## The no-project open list
`Open now: {k} programs. The five nearest:` then five sentences, then `Tell me what you build, or its repo link, and I match it.`

## track lines
Every builder line from track keeps its meaning and moves to the same voice: `Sent {short title}.`, `Tracked: that rule fits you.`, `Noted: {short title} said no.`, `{short title} is back in your list.`, `Started {short title}. {next step sentence}.`; the Next line as above.
