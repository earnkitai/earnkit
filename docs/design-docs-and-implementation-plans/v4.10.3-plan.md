**Spec:** The CTO's report (quoted under each task) and the owner's answers on 2026-11-03:

## Rulings (controller, 2026-11-03; the owner can reverse them)

- R1: An investor program closing within 30 days (`INVESTOR_NAME_MS`) is named in the money line with its own amount and kind ("a $400K accelerator") and its close ("Alliance closes in 15 days"). At most 1 investor program is named, the soonest to close. It is never in `worthUpTo` or the grant total, and never the move: v4.8.1 R1's 7-day move window stays.
- R2: When the ready set is empty, the also-open lead opens with the money line: "Open for you now: up to $X in grants" (the grant total the builder could pursue: the ready set, plus also-open, minus not-eligible and long shots), then the investor clause. If the grant total is 0, it opens with the investor clause alone, or with no money line at all.
- R3: The also-open lead names Base's first round (at most 4 answers, `pathRound` order), plus "and N more" when more remain. A yes to the lead that also answers those fields banks them as round 1. `ready.programs`-style per-field asks go to the agent for the lead too.
- R4: `UPDATING_LINE` is removed from builder text everywhere. Numbers are still left out while updating. The agent's `numbers_note` stays. The gate's `noRecord` exemption keys off the `updating` flag, not the line.
- R5: One score. The Monday block shows the live score and the live streak, with "This week" as its table header. A change since last week may show as "up N", but last week's number is never shown. `Standing.soFar` and its copy go away. The streak count stays as the live streak counts it today (this week counts once it has commits).
- R6: The Monday block has no question. On the first settled reply of the week it adds last week to the page (the action a yes to MOMENT_ASK took), says "Last week is on your page.", and asks nothing. `share` leaves `stateAsk`. The program ask (apply, the also-open lead, or the path move) is the one easy reply.
- R7: Arbitrum keeps `["arbitrum", "robinhood"]`. Correct `architecture-guide.md:9455`, and add a note under v4.10.2 R4 saying it was superseded.
