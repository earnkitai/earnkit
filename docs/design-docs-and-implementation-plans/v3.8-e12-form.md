## Flow

- Sign-in lands on /me as before. With `form` on and no `record.consent`, /me redirects to `/me/start`.
- `/me/start` (page, off: 404): one consent step with four ticks (fill forms, submit on a yes, show public work on program pages,
  public corpus and benchmark use), "Are you 18 or over?" (required), wallet (optional). No taste questions. Prefilled
  from the saved answers when changing them.
- `POST /me/consent` (route, the /me guard: form type, size, same origin, session, csrf): `action=save` writes
  `record.consent { fill, submit, list, corpus, adult, wallet?, at, version }`; `action=withdraw` (from /me) turns all
  four off and stamps `withdrawn_at`. A missing age or a long wallet goes back to `/me/start?e=adult|wallet` (fixed
  keys, never typed text).
- Under 18 (or submit not ticked): submit, list and corpus are stored off and `policy.agent_may_submit` and
  `auto_apply` are turned off, so no agent submits. Not listed and not in the corpus follow from the stored flags.
- Silent enrichment: saving fills `person.name` from the GitHub name when it is empty.
- `consent` is EarnKit's own field: not a merge block, so an agent's `record` patch with it is "not a field".
- `/privacy`: what is public, what stays private, what the yes covers, how to change your mind (decision 28).
- Delete: with `form` on, `deleteBuilder` also deletes the person row (and unpoints its installations). Off, the
  person keeps its row without the builder, as before.
