# v3.8: reply caps with `reply_fields` and `screen_v2` on

Approval: earnkitai/earnkit#159. Fixes two flags-on gate failures; flags off, every reply is main's byte for byte.

## The bugs

- `reply_fields` (E13): `mcp.ts` adds `status` and `state` after the tool has already capped its reply, so a find
  reply trimmed to just under its cap went over it (the full list: 30751 bytes against `FIND_CAP_ALL` 30720). The
  record tool's `RECORD_REPLY_CAP` had the same gap.
- `screen_v2` (E18): a results page has no byte cap; `PAGE_SIZE` 10 kept main's page under 20 KB. The flag adds
  `ordinal`, `ordinal_of` and `ordinal_reasons` to every applicant, about 50 bytes each before any reason, and the
  200-row fixture's page went to 20843 bytes.

## The fix

- `reply-fields.ts`: `REPLY_FIELDS_BYTES`, the most the two keys add (a comma and the longest status and state), and
  `replyFieldsBudget()` (that with the flag on, 0 off). `capFind` and `recordTool` cap at their cap minus the budget,
  so the reply plus `status` and `state` stays under the cap. Off the budget is 0 and the caps are main's.
- `screen-jobs.ts`: `RESULTS_CAP` 20480 and `RESULTS_ENVELOPE` 1024 (the reply around a page: builder line, agent
  note, job_id, next_page, status and state, at their longest with room). With places on the page, `fitOrdinals`
  admits the ordinal bytes only under `RESULTS_CAP - RESULTS_ENVELOPE`: the reasons go first, then the places, the last
  applicant on the page first, so the page's order holds (an applicant with no place sorts after those with one). With
  every place gone the page is main's, which `PAGE_SIZE` already fits. A cut sets `ordinals_cut: true` and the
  screen tool's agent note says `ORDINALS_CUT_NOTE`. Pages stay fixed at 10 (page numbers and `next_page` unchanged).
- No cap was raised. Flag off: `replyFieldsBudget()` is 0 and `jobResults` has no places, so nothing new runs.

## Tests

- `find.test.ts`: with `reply_fields` on, the full and default lists under 30 KB and the over-cap programs under
  20 KB, unlinked and linked (fails on main with 30751); `capFind` leaves `REPLY_FIELDS_BYTES` on, nothing off.
- `screen-jobs.test.ts`: with `screen_v2` on, the 200-row page with status and state under 20 KB, places kept as a
  prefix, `ordinals_cut` set (fails on main with 20843); a small page keeps every place and reason.
