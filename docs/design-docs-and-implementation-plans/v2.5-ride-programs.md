# v2.5: Ride external programs, coins by default, builder bonus

Status: draft for review (2026-10-05). Builds on v2.4 (`main` 6c176e0).

## Goal

Get builders to install EarnKit with a hook they care about, then earn from their coins:
- **The hook:** EarnKit matches a builder to the open money they can win in programs that already draw builders (Base, Arc and others).
- **Coins:** every project entered through EarnKit gets a coin by default.
- **Builder bonus:** coin trading fees fund a per-program builder bonus.
- **EarnKit's revenue:** 30% of coin fees now; sponsor programs later.

## Decisions (from the owner, 2026-10-05)

- **No EarnKit-funded campaigns** until a sponsor funds one in USDC through the escrow (EarnKit takes 10%). Campaign #1 stays closed.
- **The hook:** "EarnKit matches you to $X in open money".
  - With no repo: programs ranked by what a builder could win, plus build ideas from past winners and the ecosystem's focus.
- **Coins:** a coin at submission by default, named after the project, with a one-word opt-out. The builder bonus is only for entries that launch their coin through EarnKit; most programs have public submission pages, so most entries qualify.
  - Never pushy, and never advertised outside the submission step.
- **Fee split for coins launched through EarnKit**, of the pool left after Clanker's 20% cut:

  | Recipient | Share |
  |---|---|
  | Project | 40% |
  | MINI buyback | 10% |
  | Builder bonus pool | 20% |
  | EarnKit Safe (`0xc8A889577446153e28D42254B75238Ae31EC7D57`) | 30% |

  Coins launched before v2.5 keep their split.
- **Builder bonus, one round per external program.**
  - The publicly visible name is "builder bonus", never "vault".
  - It pays from day one: the owner accepted the legal risk.
- **Ranking is by real traction** from several cheap signals. Coin market cap only breaks ties; trading volume never counts.
- **After a program ends**, a coin's bonus-pool share goes to the Safe. There is no rollover.
- **Naming:** a program's factual name plus "EarnKit isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the organizer". Never logos, never "official", never the program's name in the bonus's own name.
- **Trust:** a public policy page.
- **Stats:** live stats on jenil.ai.
- **Cost:** running EarnKit stays near zero:
  - the existing Railway service and Postgres;
  - GitHub Actions for scheduling;
  - free public RPCs, explorers and the GitHub API;
  - no new paid services.

## 1. The first message (`list_programs`)

**With repos that fit:**

```
EarnKit matches you to $5,600 in open money.

| Program | Prize | Builder bonus | Ends | Your repo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base Builder Grant Program | up to $5,000 | 0.42 ETH so far | rolling | tipjar |
| Arc Microgrants | 500 USDC | 0.18 ETH so far | Oct 14 | tipjar |

Want me to start on Base Builder Grant Program with tipjar?
```

- **$X** = the sum of the top per-winner prize of each program a repo fits (`max_award_usd`). It is never the total of whole pools. Programs without a stated per-winner amount aren't counted in $X, and their table cell shows their own words.
- **The builder bonus column** shows the open round's pool so far, in ETH (WETH). The bonus is shown only for programs whose round is open.
- **Order:** "Since you last checked" (wins and bonus payouts), then the matched table, then other open programs (at most 5), then one offer.

**With no repo (none reported, none found):**

```
Open money right now:

| Program | Prize | Builder bonus | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| … ranked by prize per winner … |

Ideas that fit these programs:
- Base: a payments mini app for creators (past winners: tipping, subscriptions)
- Arc: a USDC payroll tool for small teams
- …

Want me to build one?
```

- Ideas come from each program file's new `ideas` field (at most 3 per program, curated, under 12 words each, citing past-winner themes where the program publishes winners). They are never generated freely at request time.

## 2. Coin at submission

- The v2.3 flow is unchanged: a default row at submission, an explicit symbol, `builder_confirmed`, the ownership gate, one coin per repo, and a submission that never fails because of the coin.
- The coin is named after the project; the ticker comes from v2.3's proposal rules.
- **tokens.fun change.** For launches authenticated with EarnKit's key (v2.3 `EARNKIT_DEPLOY_API_KEY`), the reward recipients are, in this order:
  1. **Platform: the Safe**, 3000 bps. This keeps index 0 = platform, so the tokens.fun revenue indexer keeps working. Its revenue view counts slot 0.
  2. **Creator: the builder's wallet**, 4000 bps.
  3. **MINI buyback wallet**, 1000 bps.
  4. **Builder bonus wallet**, 2000 bps.
- **Admins:** each slot's admin is the Safe, except the creator's own slot, which keeps the creator as admin. A leaked bonus-wallet key can then never redirect future fees.
- **Program link:** EarnKit records which program a coin was launched for: `projects.program_id` already holds it, and the launch stores the token address.

## 3. Builder bonus

**Rounds**
- A program with a window (`starts_at`/`ends_at`) is one round, open while the program is open.
- A rolling program (no `ends_at`) has calendar-month rounds.

**Pool accounting**
- One bonus wallet receives the 20% share from every EarnKit coin.
- WETH is attributed to rounds by coin. The FeeLocker balance is per recipient, not per coin, so attribution happens when fees are collected:
  1. The cron calls Clanker's LP locker `collectRewards(coin)` for each EarnKit coin. Anyone can call it.
  2. It reads that transaction's FeeLocker deposit event for the bonus wallet (the same deposit-to-slot attribution the tokens.fun fee indexer uses) and books the amount to the round of the program the coin was entered in, open at collection time.
  3. It then claims the bonus wallet's WETH (`claim(bonusWallet, WETH)`).
- Fees claimed after that program's round has ended go to the Safe. A daily sweep sends them from the bonus wallet to the Safe.

**Eligibility.** An entry is eligible for a program's bonus when all of these hold:
1. It was submitted through EarnKit for that program, with its coin launched. In EarnKit's flow those are one step: skipping the coin means no builder bonus, and the submission's coin row says so in one short line ("skip: no builder bonus").
2. There's ownership proof: the repo's `earnkit.txt` names the payout wallet, using the v2.3/v2.4 rules.
3. There's submission proof: a public URL on the program's own submission site that EarnKit fetches and that links the entry's repo or live link.
   - Each program file gets a `submission_proof` pattern (for example DoraHacks BUIDL pages).
   - Programs whose submissions aren't public (Google Forms and similar) show "builder bonus not available".

**Traction score.** Every signal counts only activity inside the round. Signals are normalized within the round (rank-based) and combined with weights:

| Signal | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Real onchain users (v2.4 rules) | 40 | Blockscout + Base RPC |
| Live product works (share of daily checks the live link loaded) | 20 | v2.3 safe link probe |
| Shipping activity (commit days, contributors) | 20 | GitHub API |
| New repo interest (stars and forks from accounts older than 30 days, capped) | 10 | GitHub API |
| Coin holders excluding the builder and EarnKit wallets (capped) | 10 | Blockscout |
| Coin market cap | Tie-breaker only | onchain or DefiLlama |

**Payout**
- At the round's end, scores freeze and the top 10 are public for 48h with the owner's veto (v2.4 review page).
- The pool is then paid automatically in WETH from the bonus wallet: 25, 18, 14, 11, 9, 7, 6, 4, 3, 3% (renormalized when fewer than 10 are eligible).
- The v2.4 payout engine is reused: frozen terms, sanctions screen, idempotent sends, and the 503 to the owner.
- One payout per payout wallet and per installation per round.

**Money flow is automatic**
- The cron claims the Safe's 30% from the FeeLocker for every EarnKit coin, daily. Anyone can trigger a claim, and the fees land in the Safe.
- It claims the bonus wallet's 20% too.
- No key except the bonus wallet's is on Railway, and that wallet holds only unpaid round pools.

## 4. Trust and stats

- **A public policy page** (`/policy`, linked from the footer and from every bonus mention):
  - how matching works;
  - the fee split;
  - how the bonus is funded, ranked and paid;
  - what counts as traction;
  - the review and veto;
  - no affiliation with any program or organizer;
  - the coin is optional.
- **A public stats endpoint** (`/api/stats`, cached 10 min): open money matched, coins launched, fees to the Safe, bonus paid, and installs. It is for jenil.ai to show.

## 5. Cost

- **Scheduling:** the v2.4 hourly GitHub Actions workflow (free). Bonus scoring runs daily per open round, and hourly in the last day.
- **Reads:** at most one GitHub API call set per entry per day, with the authenticated token's free 5,000/h. Blockscout and Base RPC reads are bounded per entry and cached.
- **FeeLocker claims:** daily per coin, only when the claimable amount exceeds the gas cost by at least 10×. Otherwise wait.
- **Gas** comes from the bonus wallet (Base gas is fractions of a cent).
- **No new services and no new paid tiers.**

## 6. What's removed or kept

- **Campaign #1 (EarnKit-funded):** kept closed. The v2.4 campaign code stays, and sponsor campaigns can reuse it later.
- **v2.4 flows kept:** the alerts engine, the identity per OAuth client, entry v2 and the scoring engine (reused as the "real onchain users" signal).

## Out of scope

- Non-Base coins.
