Requirements

How to participate

Participating projects must meet the following conditions:

  • Projects must build within the TapeOut ecosystem and either use TapeOut circuits, contracts, or onchain data on X Layer, or run on X Layer DeWEB.
  • Eligible directions include, but are not limited to, apps, games, online services, developer tools, circuit design and projects exploring mechanisms such as PoD. Projects addressing real needs of Web2 and Web3 users are encouraged. Building on existing ecosystem tools and applications is welcome.
  • Project descriptions must explain the product or service, target users and how to reach them, the proposed revenue model, and why users would pay and keep using the product. Circuit, tool and mechanism projects may explain the use cases and commercial applications they support.
  • Circuit projects must submit the processor contract address and circuit ID. Websites, apps and tools must provide an access link and explain which TapeOut ecosystem projects or features they use.
  • Existing projects are eligible but must include substantial new development completed during the event and describe what was added.
  • Individuals and teams are welcome. The hackathon takes place online, with shortlisted teams attending offline Demo Day.

Judging Criteria

Judging criteria

The official X Layer and TapeOut team will jointly evaluate entries and determine the final rankings based on the following criteria:

  • User Value and Commercial Potential: A clear understanding of real user needs and target markets, with potential for long-term growth and sustainable operations.
  • Innovation: Originality in circuit design, product features, or user experience, as well as the potential to expand TapeOut into new use cases.
  • Ecosystem Integration: Effective use of TapeOut or ecosystem capabilities such as DeWEB, FabX, TapeCode, TapeKit, and TapeSend to create meaningful product value. The use of any specific project or tool is not required.
  • Product Readiness and User Experience: Functional core features, a complete user journey, and a reduced technical barrier for users.
  • Demo Video: The recommended length is no more than three minutes. It should cover the problem being solved, a demonstration of the core features, the target users and business model, and how the project uses TapeOut or related ecosystem tools
